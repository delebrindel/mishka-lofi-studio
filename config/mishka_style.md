## Official Mascot Reference: Mishka

**The uploaded reference image is the definitive visual reference for Mishka.** All future illustrations, animations, thumbnails, profile pictures, banners, and promotional assets must preserve her recognizable appearance.

### Character Identity

Mishka is a fluffy, medium to large spitz type dog with a gentle, thoughtful, slightly melancholic personality. She is a dog, never a cat, wolf, fox, or other animal.

### Physical Appearance

- **Breed and silhouette:** Spitz type, similar to a Siberian Husky or Finnish Lapphund, with upright triangular ears, a long tapered canine muzzle, a black nose, and a large, abundant, fluffy tail.
- **Eyes:** Striking icy blue eyes, expressive and calm, with a soft, contemplative gaze.
- **Coat pattern:** Cream and white fur on the muzzle, cheeks, throat, chest, belly, and lower legs. Charcoal gray and black saddle markings across the back and shoulders. Warm apricot and golden tan fur on the crown, ears, and hindquarters.
- **Fur texture:** Thick, layered, soft, medium to long fur, with visible strands and a naturally fluffy silhouette.
- **Expression:** Gentle, intelligent, cozy, introspective, and quietly wistful. Never aggressive or overly cartoonish.

### Official Art Direction

The visual identity combines cozy lo fi illustration, cinematic lighting, nostalgic atmosphere, and delicate hand painted details. Preferred scenes include rainy windows, warm wooden interiors, soft amber lamps, muted blue evening light, books, blankets, plants, and quiet moments of reflection.

Use warm amber and brown tones indoors, balanced with cool blues and muted purples outside. Keep the mood peaceful, intimate, nostalgic, and slightly melancholic.

### Consistency Rules

1. Always use the uploaded Mishka reference image as the primary character reference.
2. Preserve her canine anatomy, upright ears, long muzzle, icy blue eyes, coat markings, and fluffy tail.
3. Never replace her with a cat or give her feline features, whisker pads, a short feline muzzle, or cat ears.
4. Never arbitrarily change her fur colors, markings, eye color, body proportions, or signature silhouette.
5. Adapt the pose, expression, camera angle, and environment as needed, while keeping Mishka recognizable.
6. Avoid generic dog designs. Character fidelity takes priority over decorative details.
7. For profile pictures and thumbnails, keep her face and eyes clearly visible, with enough space around her head for circular cropping.
8. When a new image conflicts with the official reference, follow the official reference rather than the new image.

**Priority rule:** Character consistency comes first. Every new asset must look like the same Mishka, even when the art style, lighting, setting, or pose changes.
